Tender owner's card

{{ companyName }}
Print and pin at the desk
Tender ______________________________
Closing date ____ / ____ / ______
You own this tender end to end. You pull support. You do not hand it over.
WHAT YOU DO: STAGES 00 TO 07
00Check your assigned source channels on cadence. Log anything relevant.
01Register the tender in the tracker the day you find it. Put your name on it.
02Run the go/no-go tree in order. Get gate 1 signed before spending anything.
03Register on the portal, pay the fee, book the briefing, diarise every deadline.
04Read the whole document. Build the returnables checklist and requirements matrix.
05Pull every statutory doc from the Vault. Check each expiry against the closing date.
06Attend the site visit where required. File the certificate. Submit queries in writing before the deadline.
07Price the bill from the Rate Library. Show your build-ups and your margin position.
WHAT YOU DO: STAGES 08 TO 15
08Write the method statement, programme, org chart, safety file and CV pack.
09Walk the reviewer through the returnables checklist. Get gate 2 signed.
10Assemble the full set in the tender's own order. Number and index it.
11Present final rates and totals for gate 3. No submission without a signature.
12Hand the pack to a second person for gate 4 QA. You may not review your own bid.
13Submit on the buffer day, not the closing day. Confirm the upload completed.
14File the receipt or acknowledgement. Update the tracker the same day.
15Record the outcome. Push rates to the Library and lessons to the Log: win or lose.
WHAT YOU ESCALATE: SAME DAY
A Vault document expires before the closing date
A mandatory returnable we cannot produce
Bond or guarantee beyond our facility
A JV or local-content requirement with no partner
Any gate that will be missed
Any margin below the agreed floor
FOUR SIGN-OFFS YOU MUST OBTAIN
Gate 1: bid / no-bid
Signature & date
Gate 2: compliance complete
Signature & date
Gate 3: price sign-off
Signature & date
Gate 4: two-person QA
Reviewer signature & date
